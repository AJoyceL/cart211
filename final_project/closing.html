Now you know

Now, we've reach the end of this adventure, friend. Through storm, through rain, through hurt and through pain, have I made you reflect on anything?

In a world where our clicks, movements, and choices silently fuel powerful predictions systems, staying aware of surveillance capitalism becomes an act of self-protection. By recognising how our behaviour is collected, interpreted, and monetised is the first step towards reclaiming agency in a digital environment to shape and influence us. However, awareness doesn’t mean rejecting technology –especially in an age where we depend on it in daily life– it means using it consciously and questioning what it demands from us.
